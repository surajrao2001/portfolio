## The temptation to overbuild

It is easy to decorate a personal site with auth, a CMS, a queue, and a dashboard nobody will open twice. That stack is impressive on a whiteboard and dead weight in production.

## What this site actually needs

- Real project write-ups and posts that ship with the repo
- Fast pages that index cleanly
- One honest dynamic path: the contact form

Everything else is presentation and judgment — motion systems, voice, and SEO that do not require a backend service mesh.

## A rule that keeps the codebase readable

If a feature cannot name a user-visible reason for existing, it does not ship. That is why content is MDX on disk: Git is the CMS, review is the publish button.

```md
---
title: Example post
date: 2026-01-18
---

Write it. Commit it. Deploy it.
```

<Callout title="Working rule" tone="tip">
Specific and self-aware beats generic and try-hard — in architecture as much as in copy.
</Callout>
